No Health Insurance and Mental Distress in NYC ZIP Codes

The regression analysis shows a statistically significant relationship between lack of health insurance and mental distress in NYC ZIP codes. The p-value is below 0.05, and the correlation coefficient is +0.77, which indicates a strong positive association.

This means ZIP codes with higher rates of uninsured residents tend to have higher levels of mental distress. In other words, as the percentage of people without health insurance increases, mental distress also tends to rise.

Interpretation: Although this result does not prove that lack of insurance directly causes mental distress, it suggests that access to healthcare and community well-being are closely connected. ZIP codes with more uninsured residents may also face greater barriers to care, support, and wellness resources.

What it means for people:

There is a positive relationship between being uninsured and experiencing mental distress. Communities with fewer resources and reduced access to care may be at greater risk for poor mental health outcomes.
