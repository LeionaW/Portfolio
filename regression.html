No Health Insurance and Mental Distress in NYC ZIP Codes

The p-value shows that this data is statistically relevant because it is below 0.05, and it has a strong correlation value at +0.77. This means for every 1% increase in smoking there is a 0.49% increase in mental distress.

What it means for people:

There is a positive correlation between smoking and mental distress. The more an individual smokes, the more mental distress they will feel.
