This Study Uses a Repeated Cross-Sectional Design

This study uses a repeated cross-sectional design because a new random sample of adults is surveyed each year, and the same individuals are not followed over time.

Researchers collected data from different groups of adults between 2014 and 2023 to examine trends in anxiety symptoms.

The results showed that anxiety symptoms increased among adults ages 18 to 25, rising from 8.1% in 2014 to 14.7% in 2023, while rates among adults ages 50 to 64 remained about the same.

Since the survey collected data from different participants each year rather than tracking the same people, it is considered a repeated cross-sectional study rather than a longitudinal study.
